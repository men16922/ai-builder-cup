Aide

Be the boss. Hand the work to an agent crew from your phone and make only the big decisions.

The problem

  • Production trouble does not wait for you to reach a desk.
  • Finding the cause means opening several consoles, comparing revisions, reading logs, and then deciding.
  • The decision is small. The work around it is not, and it ties a person to a laptop.

The idea

One sentence from a phone. A crew of Gemini agents does the investigation. You are asked only for the decision that is yours.

  • Ask in the assistant panel or the top bar
  • Watch each agent's step stream in
  • Decide on one approval card
  • Verify in an honest report

A real console, not a chat box

KPI tiles, traffic split, responses and latency per revision, error logs, and the crew in a right-hand Gemini panel.

Why a model cannot talk its way into a change

  • Typed tools only. No credentials, no generated code.
  • Signed approval. An HMAC over the exact action, five minutes, verified by the one service that can change anything.
  • Two independent checks. Policy before asking, policy and signature again before acting. Permission covers one service.
  • Honest about the unknown. Empty metrics are unknown, never zero. Log text is data, not instructions. Nothing is retried automatically.

Impact we can show

  • From instruction to a reviewed decision in about 44 seconds (median of three runs, bad release).
  • The person taps once. The crew performed the reads, the comparison and the verification.
  • Every step is visible and every change is attributable to an approval.

Timings are from our evaluation runs, not a benchmark against manual work.
