# Demo script — My Aide (≈2:45)

For the re-recorded judging video. Use the deployed site and the Android build of the same commit, a fresh synthetic vehicle, Korean or English consistently, and no private identifiers on screen (sign-in account chooser, approver IDs and service URLs stay off camera). Every vehicle value and caller is simulated; say so once at the start and let the on-screen SIM labels carry it afterwards.

| Time | Screen | Action | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Sign-in | Show the hero, then sign in off camera | "Aide gives every driver a personal aide: My Aide. The car here is a simulation; the AI, maps and agents are real." |
| 0:12 | Vehicle home | Ask your own Aide: "출발 전에 20도로 시원하게 해줘" | "I ask in my own words. My Aide proposes; nothing runs until I tap." Tap *Set cabin to 20°* and *Turn on climate*; the climate tile shows the cabin cooling. |
| 0:35 | Vehicle home | Lock tile, charge limit slider | "The everyday remote controls drivers expect." |
| 0:45 | Charging & map | Charging → *Let Aide handle it* | "Gemini turns my request into a search; results are real Google places with photos and attribution, numbered on the map, with my battery on arrival." Tap *Go here*. |
| 1:05 | Map while driving | *Try an incoming call* → Team lead | "A call comes in while I'm driving." Tap *Let My Aide answer*. |
| 1:15 | Call sheet | Let it speak; type one reply; *Let My Aide reply*; end | "My Aide answers, the car keeps driving on autopilot simulation, and the phone badge rides on the map." |
| 1:40 | Call summary | Show summary; *Send message*; *Save summary* | "When the call ends I get a summary and drafts. Sending or adding to my calendar is always my tap." |
| 1:55 | Vehicle diagnosis | Simulator → *Low tire pressure* | "When something goes wrong, the cluster shows where. My Aide investigates read-only and offers a service-shop search; it never invents a fix." |
| 2:15 | Error popup | (optional) show *View manual* | "Errors explain themselves; uncertain outcomes are never resent." |
| 2:25 | Platform & A2A (operator) | Show architecture, live Agent Card, guardrails | "Behind it, Chief coordinates Diagnosis, Guardian and Ops over A2A. Every change needs a Guardian verdict and a single-use human approval." |
| 2:40 | Android | Same vehicle on the phone | "The same My Aide on Android." |

Recording checklist: hide bookmarks and extensions; 1440×900 browser; phone mirrored at native resolution; keep Gemini latency in (do not speed-cut a reply into looking instant); keep one take of the call flow uncut.
